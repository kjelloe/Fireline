FIRELINE COMMAND

A drop-in, combined-arms war that runs whether you are there or not. Pick a side, take a hull, turn the front. / Velg side, ta et kjøretøy, snu fronten.

Your first 60 seconds: join a team → you are auto-seated in a vehicle wearing the green diamond → click (or tap) the ground to drive, click a visible enemy to fire. The in-game tutorial takes over from there on your first war — let it. / Klikk bakken for å kjøre, klikk en synlig fiende for å skyte. Opplæringen tar deg videre.

How you win

  • Bleed their tickets: hold the majority of relay masts and destroy their hulls — every wreck costs its owner a ticket (recovered wrecks refund it).
  • Or steal their Command Standard (the cone in their base) and carry it home in a carrier while your own standard stands.
  • Rescues, tow-backs, and resupply pay more recognition than kills — the scoreboard celebrates people who bring their team home.

The five things veterans know

  • Supply is life. Relay masts project supply; out of supply you crawl and cannot fire. Idle in base to rearm.
  • Nobody dies for long. Wrecks can be towed home and repaired; downed crews crawl, get carried, or redeploy (R). Bring them home.
  • The fog is honest. You see what your team spots — smoke hides, radar extends, storms shrink everyone's eyes.
  • Every chassis has a job. Trucks tow and build, scouts spot and mark mines, carriers rescue and run the standard, artillery breaks sites open.
  • Talk with pings. 1 2 3 (or the on-screen buttons) say attack / defend / rally — context-aware, no typing.

Controls

Mouse/keys: click ground = move · SHIFT-click = waypoint · click enemy = fire · F follow · R redeploy · T tow · B/U board/leave · J man a gun seat · G direct drive (arcade mode).
Touch: tap = order · hold ~1s = waypoint · drag = pan · pinch = zoom · the bottom-right key bar shows exactly what your current vehicle can do — gray means "not right now", blinking means "do this".

Modes & votes

Between wars everyone votes the next map and mode: classic standard war, Convoy Escort (walk one truck through their lines), Heist (steal the Asset), or a night war where every sensor is halved. Gold-starred mission cards mark objectives you personally have not tried yet.
