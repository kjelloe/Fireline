MORE FIREPOWER — Version 2 Plan

Written 2026-07-26 · marker-0038 · markdown twin: plan-version2.md · companion: plan-version1.html

V1 delivers the Command Standard war. V2 is everything else the design documents describe — five tracks compiled from specs/01–06, the phase 5–7 plans, specs/future/FUTURE_ROADMAP.md, the art spec, and user requests. Tiers: V2.0 next coherent release · V2.x after it · HORIZON documented, unscheduled.

Track A — Roster & roles completion

FeatureTierWhat it is
Command CarrierV2.0 Rescue transport: extracts downed operators, moves specialists/POWs, takes over standard-carrying. Consequential takeovers need confirmation.
Downed operatorsV2.0 Disabled asset → operator on foot: fast redeploy (~10 s), Carrier rescue, or ~60 s auto-return; can call for help, crawl to cover. The heart of the recovery fantasy.
Sentinel (Warden)V2.x Deployable defensive platform — anchor/deploy-lock area control.
Infiltrator (Freehold)V2.x Amphibious EW raider: water lanes, sensor disruption, sabotage. Needs Track B water + sensors.
Factions: Warden vs FreeholdV2.x Faction identity (unique assets, audio timbre, visual trim) replacing plain team A/B.
NPC infantry layerV2.x AI-only foot units: site guards, engineer crews, squads, specialists/POWs, civilians. Never player-micromanaged.

FeatureTierWhat it is
MinesV2.0 Assault Vehicle deploys, Scout detects; authoritative minefields demanding clearance work.

Sites beyond relaysV2.0 Depots and repair sites with damaged/restorable states; Minimum Playability Guarantee (Slow Manufacture reserve).
Route graph & congestionV2.x Node/edge network with states open/slowed/blocked/mined/damaged/contested; fast vs safer routing.

Fog ghostsV2.x Fading last-known contacts; discovered terrain stays known. Presentation of legitimate knowledge only.
Convoy eventsV2.x Escort/interdict convoys as public tasks.

Suggested V2.0 cut

“The Rescue Update” — Command Carrier + downed operators + cargo/depot economy + mines + pings/public tasks + takeover confirmations, wrapped in the painted art pass (Slices B–E + camera), with spectator/replay viewer, profiles/leaderboard, and the direct-control homage mode.

Completes the spec's core promise — “the defining experience is recovery” — using seams the engine already hosts. Estimated 12–16 slices at the established cadence.

Known tensions for the designer

  1. Lobbies (phase plan 5E) vs the no-lobby philosophy — recommend dropping 5E for the balance-aware battle picker.
  2. Artillery is in-engine but not in the spec roster — keep, rename, or fold into the Assault Vehicle family when factions land?
  3. Standard carrying — Carrier-exclusive (spec leaning) or any-chassis-with-penalty (today's small-player-count-friendly rule)?
  4. Helicopters (original Firepower's anti-camping pressure) have no spec equivalent — does a QRF/harassment event fill that emotional slot?
