To keep 32–64 players engaged for a full 30-minute block without the "mid-game slump," you can draw inspiration from the **Golden Age of LANs (late 90s/early 2000s)** and genres like **RTS, Tactical Shooters, and early MMO-PvP.**

Beyond the standard capture points, here are several "high-engagement" mechanics that maintain tension:

### 1. The "Meta-Resource" Economy (Inspiration: *Red Alert/Company of Heroes*)
In a standard FPS, resources (ammo/grenades) are infinite or timed. For a 30-minute game, adding a shared team resource pool adds a strategic "RTS" layer.
*   **Fuel/Salvage Points:** Specific capture points on the map provide "Salvage" over time. The team uses this to "buy" heavy vehicle spawns or base defenses. 
*   **Player Impact:** If infantry players feel the enemy tanks are too strong, they can pivot to "resource raiding" (destroying the enemy’s fuel trucks or harvesters). This gives non-combat roles (drivers, engineers) a massive sense of purpose.

### 2. Base Building and Fortification (Inspiration: *Natural Selection / Squad*)
*Natural Selection* (a 2002 *Half-Life* mod) was the king of this. One player is the "Commander" in a top-down view, but the FPS players have to actually build the structures.
*   **Dynamic Cover:** In a 30-minute match, the map should look different at minute 25 than it did at minute 1. Allowing players to build sandbags, wire, or "Trophy Systems" (anti-grenade) at capture points gives the defending team a "home turf" advantage.
*   **The "Forward Operating Base" (FOB):** Instead of fixed spawns, let players create their own. This makes the game about "search and destroy" missions to find the enemy’s hidden rally points.

### 3. Asymmetric Objective Phases (Inspiration: *Wolfenstein: Enemy Territory*)
*Enemy Territory* (2003) mastered the "Multi-Stage" objective. Instead of just "holding flags," the game is a linear story:
*   **Phase 1:** Blow up the sea wall (5 mins).
*   **Phase 2:** Steal the gold/documents from the vault (15 mins).
*   **Phase 3:** Escort the truck to the extraction point (10 mins).
*   **Why it's fun:** It prevents "stalemate fatigue." Even if you are losing, if you hold the sea wall for 4 minutes and 59 seconds, you feel a massive sense of accomplishment before moving to the next zone.

### 4. Direct "Hero" or "V.I.P." Rewards (Inspiration: *Star Wars: Battlefront / OG Unreal Tournament*)
In a 64-player match, individual performance can feel lost. 
*   **The Killstreak Asset:** Similar to the original *Battlefront*, the top-performing players (or a random lucky player) could be granted access to a "Hero Asset"—a heavy bomber, a massive "Bolo" tank, or a specialized prototype weapon. 
*   **Mutators:** Introduce "Mid-Game Events." At the 15-minute mark, a "Sandstorm" or "Night Fall" occurs, forcing everyone to switch from long-range snipers to close-quarters thermal optics. This "resets" the flow of the match.

### 5. Multi-Crew "Social" Vehicles (Inspiration: *Starsiege: Tribes / Planetside*)
*Tribes* (1998) proved that vehicles are more fun when they require teamwork.
*   **The Mobile Fortress:** Instead of 10 solo tanks, give the team one "Landship" or "Galaxy" transport that requires 1 pilot, 4 gunners, and 2 repair engineers. 
*   **Why it works:** It creates "mini-communities" inside the 32-man team. Those 7 people are now playing their own "game within a game," and their survival depends on each other.

### 6. The "Environmental" Factor (Inspiration: *Battlefield 4 "Levolution" / SimCity*)
For a 30-minute match, the environment should be a participant.
*   **Destruction as Strategy:** If a bridge is the only way for tanks to get to Point B, the losing team should be able to blow it up. This forces the winning team to stop their momentum and wait for engineers to repair it or find a flanking naval route.
*   **Power Grids:** Capturing a "Power Plant" objective could disable the enemy's automated base turrets or night-vision capabilities, creating a "blackout" window for a stealth assault.

### 7. "Dead Man's Hand" / The Final Stand (Inspiration: *Titanfall / Last Stand*)
The most boring part of a 64-player game is the last 3 minutes when one team is obviously losing by 400 points.
*   **The Extraction Phase:** When the tickets hit zero, the "Defeated" team is given one final objective: Get to the extraction choppers.
*   **Survival Bonus:** If they escape, they get bonus XP or "Currency" for the next round. This turns a "Loss" into a tense, fun "Escaped with my life" mini-game.

### Summary of the "Pacing" Secret:
To keep a 30-minute game from feeling like a "grind," you must alternate between **Macro-Goals** (Win the war) and **Micro-Dramas** (Repair this specific tank, hold this specific door, escort this specific squad). If you give a player a "Job" beyond just "Click on heads," they will stay for the full 30 minutes.
