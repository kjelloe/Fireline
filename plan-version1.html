Definition of done for v1

  1. Two humans on LAN + AI regents play a complete war — contest relays, steal a standard, rescue wrecks via the truck, reach the end screen — then keep playing the next war without touching the server. (Shipped; needs playtest-3 confirmation.)
  2. A newcomer understands where they are, what they drive, and what to do inside 60 seconds. (Briefing + auto-crew + objective strip + labels shipped; playtest 2 positive.)
  3. npm test green; every mechanic hashed, replayable, fixture-pinned. (272/272.)
