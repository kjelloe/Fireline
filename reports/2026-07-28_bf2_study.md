## Gaps worth a ruling (proposed slices, cheap→deep)

1. **13H Ticket bleed** — the study's single strongest pacing claim: a
   depleting pool beats a timer for endgame tension. Ours would be:
   relay MAJORITY drains the enemy ticket pool per tick; pool empty =
   loss. We already have the points-horn; bleed would REPLACE slow
   horn-grinding wars with converging finales. Engine change, big
   balance swing — needs the full gate + a ruling on numbers.
2. **15F Carrier field-respawn** — "spawn on your squad" is the
   loneliness killer. Our natural fit: redeploy onto a crewed friendly
   CARRIER (it already has bunks + the rescue fantasy) instead of the
   base, with a cooldown. Makes carriers the beating heart of a push,
   doubles down on rescue-outranks-kills. Medium engine slice.
3. **Lateral relay pair for frontier** — the corridor map has no
   flanking objective; the study warns straight corridors snowball.
   A mirrored north/south relay pair on the trail loops (e.g. 44/83 at
   rows 40/86) gives a losing team a back-cap route. Map + layout
   change; the mirror invariant machinery makes it safe to add.
4. **AT satchel for downed crews** — "two infantry can kill a tank":
   give a downed operator ONE close-range satchel charge (heavy risk,
   heroic counterplay vs the new body-blocking). Small engine slice,
   large feel upside; interacts with POW/rescue races later.
5. **Batched MPG waves** — rebuild N hulls together on the cadence
   instead of trickling, so a gutted team counter-pushes as a WAVE.
   Tiny reducer change over 13F rules; sim-gate the tempo.
